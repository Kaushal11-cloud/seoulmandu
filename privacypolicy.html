01. Introduction

Welcome to Seoulmandu Korean Mart (“Seoulmandu,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information when you visit our website, browse our products, make online payments, or place orders through WhatsApp.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, shared, and protected when you use our website.

By using our website, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

02. Information We Collect

Seoulmandu does not require customers to create an account to browse products or place orders.

Depending on how you use our website, we may process the following information:

Product and Cart Information

  • Products selected for your cart.
  • Product quantities, prices, and order totals.
  • Cart information or state that is transferred to WhatsApp when you submit your cart.

Order and Contact Information

When you contact us or place an order through WhatsApp, you may provide your name, phone number, delivery address, order details, and other information needed to fulfill your order.

Online Payment Information

When you make an online payment, relevant payment information may be processed by our payment service provider. The information collected depends on the payment method and provider used.

We do not claim to store or access complete payment card details unless our actual payment system requires and securely handles that information.

Technical Information

Our website or its service providers may collect technical information, such as browser type, device information, IP address, and website usage data, depending on the technologies implemented on the website.

03. How We Use Your Information

We use relevant information to:

  • Display products and manage shopping cart functionality.
  • Transfer selected products and cart details to WhatsApp when you initiate an order.
  • Process and confirm online payments.
  • Coordinate order fulfillment and delivery.
  • Respond to customer inquiries and provide support.
  • Improve website performance, security, and usability.
  • Detect suspicious activity and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We aim to collect and use only the information reasonably necessary for these purposes.

04. Shopping Cart and WhatsApp Orders

Seoulmandu allows customers to select products and submit their cart details through WhatsApp.

When you use this feature, relevant cart information, such as product names, quantities, prices, and order totals, may be included in a WhatsApp message. Any additional information you choose to provide will be handled as part of the conversation.

Important points:
  • Submitting your cart through WhatsApp does not automatically mean an order has been confirmed.
  • Your order may require confirmation from Seoulmandu before it is accepted or fulfilled.
  • WhatsApp processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms.
  • We do not control how WhatsApp independently handles information on its platform.

Please verify your product selections, quantities, and total amount before confirming an order.

05. Online Payments

Our website supports online payments through the payment services made available at checkout.

When you make a payment, relevant transaction information may be processed by our payment provider to authorize, verify, and complete the transaction.

Depending on the payment method, this may include the payment amount, transaction reference, payment status, and information necessary to process the payment.

Payment providers may independently process personal information under their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

Customers should use only the official payment methods presented on our website. We do not guarantee that payment information is handled in a particular way unless confirmed by the relevant payment provider and our website's technical implementation.

06. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies to maintain shopping cart information, remember preferences, improve website functionality, and support security.

If your cart information is stored in your browser, it may remain available on the same device until it is cleared, overwritten, or removed according to the website's implementation.

You can manage cookies and certain storage data through your browser settings. Clearing this data may remove saved cart information or preferences.

We do not claim to use analytics or advertising cookies unless those technologies are implemented on our website.

07. Sharing of Information

Seoulmandu does not sell personal information.

We may share relevant information when necessary with:

  • Payment service providers: to process, verify, and manage online payments.
  • WhatsApp: when you choose to send cart information or communicate with us through the platform.
  • Delivery partners: to fulfill orders and deliver products.
  • Website and technical service providers: to operate, maintain, and secure our website.
  • Legal authorities: when disclosure is required by applicable law or a lawful request.

We aim to limit information sharing to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

08. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no online platform or electronic transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through WhatsApp or other communication channels.

09. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill orders, manage transactions, provide customer support, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

Cart information stored locally in your browser may remain there until it is cleared or removed. Information shared through WhatsApp or processed by payment providers may also be retained under their respective policies.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it where appropriate and legally permitted.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally permitted.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise concerns about how your information is handled.

To make a request, contact us using our official business contact details. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

12. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may link to or integrate with third-party services, including WhatsApp and online payment providers.

These services may have separate privacy policies and terms. Seoulmandu is not responsible for independent data-handling practices outside our control.

We encourage customers to review the privacy policies of the relevant third-party services.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, payment methods, business practices, or applicable laws.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage customers to review the policy periodically.
